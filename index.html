iPhone / Apple Calendar

  1. Tap Add to iPhone / Apple Calendar above.
  2. Tap Subscribe, then Add.

Google Calendar (Android and web)

  1. Tap Add to Google Calendar above and confirm, or on a computer open Google Calendar, click + next to "Other calendars", choose From URL and paste the calendar link.
  2. On Android, open the Calendar app, then Settings, and make sure "FeLIS Natal 2026" is ticked.

Outlook

  1. On the web (outlook.com): Calendar, Add calendar, Subscribe from web, paste the calendar link, name it "FeLIS Natal 2026" and click Import.
  2. Outlook for Windows: File, Account Settings, Internet Calendars, New, paste the link.

New events are added every week. Calendar apps check for updates on their own schedule: Google can take up to a day, Apple and Outlook usually a few hours.
